Executive BI Dashboard for Retail Performance
Overview

This project provides a complete Power BI dashboard designed to analyze retail business performance across multiple dimensions including sales, profit, products, customers, regions, stores, and sales channels. The report features interactive visuals, drill-down capabilities, and optimized DAX measures to support accurate and data-driven decision-making.

Dashboard Pages
1. Executive Overview

This page contains the high-level KPIs and visual summaries, including:

Total Net Sales

Total Profit

Average Order Value

Total COGS

Quarterly Sales Trend

Top 10 Customers

Sales and Profit by Region

Store Performance by Profit and Profit Margin

2. Sales Performance

This page focuses on overall sales metrics and trends, including:

Gross Sales vs Net Sales Over Time

Discount Percentage

Year-to-Year Growth

Profit Margin Percentage

Top 10 Store Sales

Top 5 Products Profit and Profit Margin

3. Product Performance

This page analyzes product-level insights, including:

Top 5 Products by Sales

Top 5 Products by COGS

Top 10 Products by Quantity Sold

Top Discounted Products

Total Products

Total Quantity Sold

Average Unit Price

4. Customer and Channel Analysis

This section provides insights into customer behavior and channel contribution, including:

Orders per Customer

Average Sales per Customer

Repeat Customers

Net Sales by Channel

Top Products per Channel

Orders Distribution by Channel

5. Regional and Store Analysis

This page breaks down performance by location and store, including:

Best Region by Sales

Worst Region by Sales

Top Store by Profit

Top Store by Net Sales

Net Sales by Region

Profit Margin Percentage by Region

Top 10 Stores Ranked by Net Sales

This structure enables efficient DAX calculations for Net Sales, Profit, COGS, Discount, Quantity, Growth Percentage, and Profit Margin.

Technologies Used

Power BI Desktop

DAX

Star Schema Data Modeling

Interactive Visualizations

How to Use

Open the Power BI (.pbix) file in Power BI Desktop.

Review or update data connections if required.

Refresh the dashboard.

Use the filters to explore insights by region, product, customer, store, and channel.
